#  0 Welcome to FHIR® [](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/index.html#0 "link to here")
FHIR is a standard for health care data exchange, published by HL7®.
**First time here?**  
See the [executive summary](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/summary.html), the [developer's introduction](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/overview-dev.html), [clinical introduction](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/overview-clinical.html), or [architect's introduction](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/overview-arch.html), and then the FHIR [overview / roadmap](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/overview.html) & [Timelines](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/versions.html). See also the [open license](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/license.html) (and don't miss the full [Table of Contents](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/toc.html) and the [Community Credits](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/credits.html) or you can [search this specification](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/search.cfm)).
